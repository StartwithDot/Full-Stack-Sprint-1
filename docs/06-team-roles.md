# Team Roles

Sprint 1 should teach collaboration without allowing collaboration to replace individual learning.

## Individual responsibility

Every learner practises the core skills personally.

## Shared-build responsibility

The cohort may work on the shared Poll Maker build in defined rotations.

## Review responsibility

Students should review one another's work with comments that explain:

- what is clear
- what is incorrect or incomplete
- why a change matters
- what could be made clearer

Final role names and rotation frequency will be defined with the cohort operating model.
